# Supplementary evidence and statistical archive

The reading supplement contains 17 figures and ten tables. The 700-hPa wind-matched scale-boundary sensitivity belongs to Table S5. Find the original twelve estimands under B087 (historical Table S9h), with slow cutoffs of 8, 16 and 32 days, each paired with its adjacent intermediate band. Within each band the quantities are dry available potential energy and APE-to-KE conversion. Common-support unadjusted contrasts of 105 cyclone identifiers are descriptive. The original and adjusted comparisons have separate twelve-member testing families. Use the stored intervals and the q value for each respective family. The 104-identifier layer analysis in MWT_MATCHED concerns a different layer and estimand. A catalog mention of S9h for the latter does not link it to the twelve 700-hPa comparisons.

The complete frozen result archive is optional research material: the submission SI contains the evidence needed for the reported conclusions. In Supplementary_Data_1_CSV.zip, use Table_directory.csv to look up historical table identifiers, Table_rows.csv to retrieve their printed values, Native_catalog.csv to identify retained numerical sources, and native/ for original units and precision. The companion Excel workbook reproduces the historical archive; its figure display pointers and reading-table crosswalk describe v2.1.4. For the current presentation use panel_identity.csv and the Table S5 evidence file. Blank P/q entries are unspecified, not zero. Fig. 6e displays nine rates (117 SIDs, 126 events; W kg−1), whereas Supplementary Fig. 13b displays cumulative event integrals (122 SIDs, 132 events; J kg−1).
